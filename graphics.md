## 📱 3. App Screenshots (Polishing Raw Captures)
For a premium listing, raw device screenshots should be framed within a phone mockup, set against an artistic background gradient, and labeled with descriptive feature callouts.

### Screenshot 1: Arcade Home Screen
* **How to Capture Raw**:
  1. Open the app to the **Home** (Arcade Hub) screen.
  2. Ensure your daily challenge status shows some progression (e.g., active daily streak/coins visible) to look populated.
  3. Capture the screen using `adb shell screencap -p /sdcard/home.png` and pull it.
* **AI Polishing & Framing Prompt**:
  > **Prompt**: Place this raw mobile screenshot inside a modern bezel-less smartphone mockup (like a dark slate titanium phone). The phone is tilted slightly at a 15-degree angle to create depth. The background is a beautiful, dark gradient of cosmic indigo and soft neon purple with a subtle grid pattern. At the top of the image, add clean white sans-serif text in a premium layout that reads: "YOUR OFFLINE ARCADE HUB". Add a glowing subtitle: "3 Classic Games, Zero Interruptions". The style should look like an official App Store marketing presentation, clean, polished, 3D shadow effects under the phone frame.

Volumetric glow effects, premium gaming marketing asset, high contrast.
